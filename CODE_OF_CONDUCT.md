# Code of conduct

Merx follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussions focused on building good software. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@kamilkubik89](https://github.com/kamilkubik89), through a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially.
